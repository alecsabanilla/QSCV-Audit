RACKS QSCV
Audit — field app

Working prototype of the RACKS-QSCV Audit Form as a one-handed store-walk tool. Nine weighted categories, Y / N / N-A scoring at the item's own risk level, evidence attached at the moment of the finding, and a grade that moves as you tap.

Scoring logic, as built
PointsHigh 10 · Medium 5 · Low 1. N-A items leave both actual and possible.
CategoryActual ÷ Possible, then × the category's %weight.
GradeSum of the nine weighted scores. 91–100 Excellent · 86–90 Very Good · 80–85 Good · 79 and below Unsatisfactory.
Critical failFive or more High-risk items scored N — overrides the grade.
Assumptions to confirm
  • Risk levels for Category VII (FOH) were inferred — the sheet leaves several blank.
  • Deviations map from risk: High → Critical (10), Medium → Major (5), Low → Minor (1).
  • An MOD signing in gets read-only scoring and can only acknowledge.
  • Speed standards sample 5 transactions (3 for billing / bussing) per the sheet's guides.
Try: tap N on any item — the deviation sheet opens with the severity pre-set and the score drops in the header. Everything persists locally; reload and the draft is still there.
